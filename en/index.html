About me

Hi, I'm Carlos

I am an industrial engineer from the University of Zaragoza and I have completed the accredited Master's Degree in Industrial Engineering, specialising in energy.

I spent the final year of the master's at the University of Liège (Belgium), where I did an internship in energy systems. There I modelled and simulated a CO₂-based thermal storage system, the work I defended in English as my Master's thesis.

I am interested in everything that contributes to a more efficient use of energy: how it is generated, how it is managed and how it is consumed. I am passionate about seeing how the sector is changing and I want to be part of that change in areas such as renewable generation (solar PV and wind), battery energy storage (BESS) and long-duration storage (LDES), heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC), system modelling and simulation, and building services (MEP).

My goal is to join a large company where I can learn and develop my career, both by taking on new responsibilities and by getting to know different areas.

I see myself as ambitious, curious and resilient. I am attentive and empathetic, and it matters a lot to me to feel at ease with the people I work with: I work hard to make it so.
